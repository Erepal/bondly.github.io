Bondly CSAE Prevention Standards

Bondly is committed to maintaining a safe environment for all users and has a zero-tolerance policy toward child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE).

Zero-Tolerance Policy: Any content, behavior, communication, or activity involving child sexual abuse, exploitation, grooming, trafficking, or endangerment is strictly prohibited on Bondly.

Our Commitment

Bondly is a relationship guidance platform designed to provide educational and informational content related to dating, relationships, marriage, personal growth, and emotional wellness.

We are committed to protecting children and preventing the misuse of our platform for any form of child sexual abuse or exploitation.

Prohibited Content and Activities

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM)
  • Sexual content involving minors
  • Grooming or solicitation of minors
  • Sex trafficking involving minors
  • Sexual exploitation or coercion of children
  • Content promoting or encouraging abuse of minors
  • Attempts to share, request, or distribute CSAE-related material

Reporting and Enforcement

Any user found engaging in activities related to CSAE will be immediately removed from the platform and may be reported to the appropriate law enforcement authorities and child protection organizations where required by law.

We actively review reports submitted by users and take prompt action against violations of these standards.

User Reporting

Users who encounter content or behavior that may violate these standards are encouraged to report it immediately.

  • Report suspicious content through the app support channels.
  • Email our safety team directly.
  • Provide as much relevant information as possible to assist investigations.

Compliance

Bondly complies with applicable laws and regulations relating to child safety and online protection. We cooperate with relevant authorities when legally required and appropriate.

Child Safety Measures

  • Content moderation procedures
  • User reporting mechanisms
  • Account enforcement actions
  • Ongoing review of safety policies
  • Immediate action on verified violations
